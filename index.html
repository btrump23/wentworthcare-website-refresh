Chapter 07 — Cultivate a home

Designing dementia-friendly care environments

Designing for people living with dementia isn’t just about “nice” interiors; it’s about enabling independence, reducing falls, and simplifying infection control.

Australia now has national guidance for residential aged care design (developed with Dementia Training Australia) that emphasises environments which are familiar, legible, safe, and easy to clean — furniture choices are central to that.

Chapter 09 — What to consider

Materials & Fabrics

Aged care and healthcare environments need suitable fabrics — antibacterial, antimicrobial, bleach cleanable.

Whether you are looking for an easy to wipe vinyl, or bright and bold aged care appropriate fabrics, there is a huge selection to choose from, plus a wide range of timber stains and finishes.

  • Commercial fabrics
  • Waterproof fabrics
  • Wipe-clean vinyls
  • Timber stains

Fabric suppliers referenced on the current site include Warwick, Materialised and Wortley. Always check the care label for the fabric you choose — not every fabric can be bleach cleaned.

Chapter 10 — Good to know

FAQ

Do you deliver outside Victoria?

Yes. Specialised carriers and installation teams deliver and install projects of any scale across Australia — from major cities to regional towns.

Can we trial furniture before ordering?

Where possible we provide samples and trial furniture onsite, or you can visit the Campbellfield showroom by appointment.

Can products be customised?

Yes. Choose from a wide range of stains, fabrics and finishes, and standard products can be customised. Around 90% of products are made in Australia.
